The problem

The answer's in the manual. Finding it isn't.

A service tech with a machine down has two bad options: dig through a thousand-page PDF with greasy gloves, or sit through a twelve-minute YouTube video hoping the spec is buried somewhere near the end. Both cost the one thing a shop bills for — time on the machine.

Dozer Dispatch removes the dig. You talk; it answers from your manual, cites the page, and keeps working the problem with you. The knowledge was always there. We just made it answer back.

Under the hood

Built to feel instant, engineered to scale.

The hard parts are invisible on purpose. Voice runs on a self-hosted streaming SDK with a signed-URL handshake, so a private agent talks in real time and a soft-pause mutes the mic without dropping the session or its memory. Answers are retrieved from a per-machine vector index built out of the manual's own pages, so every reply can cite where it came from — and each call is summarized back into the user's profile so the next conversation already knows the machine.

Getting there meant ingesting the manuals at scale. The original pipeline read one manual at a time on a single core. We parallelized it across processes — roughly 4× faster — added watermark scrubbing and an OCR-quality gate for rough scans, and took the shared catalog to ~696 machines across 634 cleanly-ingested manuals, spanning fifty-plus brands.
